First working prototype of Standpoint Core is running locally. Very rough, but it works.

The prototype models a simple scenario: three departments (Engineering, Marketing, Legal) coordinating on a product launch. Each department has different concerns, different metrics, different definitions of success.

What the prototype can now do:
- Model the same situation from each department's perspective
- Identify where perspectives conflict
- Surface implications that wouldn't be visible from any single viewpoint

What it can't do yet:
- Scale beyond toy examples
- Handle temporal aspects (how perspectives change over time)
- Connect to external systems

But the core concept is proven. Perspective-aware modeling isn't just theoretical - it's implementable.

Now the hard work of making it production-ready begins.
