Spent this week mapping the emerging context graph landscape. Every major AI company is racing to add organizational context to their systems.

What they're building:
- Knowledge graphs of company data
- RAG systems with enterprise documents
- Agent memory and context windows

What they're missing:
- How the same information means different things to different parties
- Implication tracking across perspectives
- Coordination beyond information sharing

This gap is exactly where Standpoint fits. We're not competing with context graphs - we're completing them.

The positioning is becoming clearer: Standpoint Labs is the perspective layer that makes context graphs actually useful for coordination.
