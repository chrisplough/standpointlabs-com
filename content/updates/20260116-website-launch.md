The website is live. Finally.

Building the site was a useful forcing function for clarifying the messaging. What exactly does Standpoint Labs do? Who is it for? Why does it matter?

Those questions are easier to answer now than they were a month ago:

**What:** Infrastructure for perspective-aware AI. Specifically: Standpoint Core (relational modeling from multiple perspectives) and Standpoint Mesh (coordination across boundaries).

**Who:** AI/ML teams deploying agent systems. Product managers navigating complex stakeholder landscapes. Enterprise leaders making decisions that ripple across organizations. Individuals managing personal AI agent teams.

**Why:** Context graphs are proliferating, but they're not solving the coordination problem. They give AI access to information without helping it understand what that information *means* to different parties. Standpoint is the missing layer.

More updates coming as we move toward design partner onboarding and seed round close.

Thanks for following along.
