What Relational Core is

Technology that lets humans and digital intelligence understand each person, and each group of people, deeply enough that how it acts, what it sees and how it helps are rooted in who we are and who we get to be. Real relationship to build on. And complete privacy, so that it can never be used against you or those you love.

Hold a faceted crystal up to the light. Twelve faces, and every one is a window onto the same interior. Turn it, and the same copper flecks rearrange. Nothing inside changed; where you stand did. Relational Core is that, built as software: a way of modelling people, groups and ideas so that what a connection means is kept for each party that has a stake in it.

The thing

A person, a team or an idea is modelled as a faceted solid

Its interior is what it seeks, what it offers, what it protects: the copper inside the crystal. That interior is one thing. It does not change when someone new looks at it.

The standpoint

Each face is a standpoint: what this connection means from there

A new hire is relief to the founder, uncertainty to a teammate, and a whole new life to the person arriving. Same interior, three faces. Most systems keep one of them and call it the truth. Relational Core keeps all three and knows which one you are asking from.

The recursion

Each face is itself a solid

The teammate who is one face of the team is a whole crystal of their own, with their own faces, their own copper. The model goes all the way down, which is why it can say what an action means to a group and to each person in it, without flattening either.

{{ partial "crystal.svg" . }}
Facets
The parties and the things they are: a person, a team, a project, an idea. Each is modelled whole, and each can be a face of another.
Predicates
What is true between facets: seeks, offers, protects, fears, owes, carries. The copper inside, made explicit.
Perspectives
What a predicate means when you ask from a particular face. The same fact, rendered for the founder, the teammate, the person arriving.

What gets built on this

Relational Core is infrastructure. Partners build the applications, and every one of them is the same layer asked from a different face. Learn the pattern once: the application asks, the layer answers per standpoint, the application acts.

Communities

the uncommon commonality: the thing two members both protect or both seek that neither has said out loud.

Introductions with the reason attached, in a mastermind, a founders' group, a cohort. Genuine opportunity instead of networking noise.

Coaches, facilitators, practitioners

"What does this person protect that they have not said?"

the standpoint they are speaking from, and the one they are avoiding.

A conversation that starts from understanding rather than guesswork, and a practitioner who can keep helping as the person changes.

Role decisions

"Where will this person thrive, and with whom?"

what they value and where they are trying to go, not only what they can do.

Hiring, promotion and pairing that place people where they will thrive and catch the mismatch before it costs a year.

We do not build these applications. We build the layer they cannot easily build themselves.

Why it is necessary

Everybody is building a brain for AI. The part that understands what relationships mean is missing, and the gap shows in three places.

Most systems hold one view and call it truth

They give AI access to information without modelling what it means, to whom, and why. The result is confident advice from nowhere in particular.

Agents have your data without your meaning

The self-hosted agent wave is real: your server, your models, your data. But agents are islands. Each acts without understanding what its actions imply for the others, or for the people they serve. Seeing without acting is a museum. Acting without seeing is a wrecking ball.

The map is the most valuable dataset about a person, and the most dangerous

The same understanding that helps someone serve you could be used against you. So the map has to live somewhere that answers to you. That is why this layer is built on sovereign infrastructure, and why the first deployment runs on hardware its founder controls: Node0, documented.

The layer, not the apps

Sovereign does not mean isolation. It means choice: hold what should be held internally, leverage frontier models where they make a meaningful difference, and let whoever holds your map answer to you. We call it connected sovereignty.
